Cosa Nostra Tycoon — Screen Tour
How to navigate
Tap the bottom nav (Empire / Family / City / War / Shop). On the Empire screen tap "Manage HQ" to see the Mansion view. On the City screen tap "RAID" on any target to trigger the battle modal.
  • 🏢 Empire (home): hero card, crew, mansion preview, businesses, war banner.
  • 🏛️ Mansion / HQ: cartoon skyline header, then 8 buildings (Hideout, Vault, Training Yard, Clinic, Garage, Armory, Walls, Speakeasy) — each upgradeable.
  • 👥 Family: your TG group as a Family — crest, TG chat link, daily Boss, member roster with R5/R4/R3 roles, contributions.
  • 🗺️ City: map with your turf (gold), enemy turfs (red/blue/green/purple), capturable + contested zones. Nearby targets list with scout/raid buttons.
  • ⚔️ War Room: active war vs Manila Predators with score bar, member contribution leaderboard, rally / solo / scout / shield actions, battle reports.
  • 🎰 Shop: Capo Pass (VIP subscription), Telegram Stars packs, utility items (shield/energy/speedup/migration), cosmetics.
  • ⚔ Raid modal: tap RAID anywhere → target preview → STRIKE → battle animation → victory/loot screen.
  • Stack reminder: all hand-coded SVG + CSS for demo. Production = PixiJS + Spine 2D rigs (much more polished art + smooth skeletal animations).
